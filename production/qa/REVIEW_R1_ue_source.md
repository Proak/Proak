- Independence: single-agent review. I am independent of the author but not a human. Per CLAUDE.md rule 10 this is **not** a substitute for a human reviewer.
- Overall verdict: **UE runtime = NOT_RUN** (no engine). **Compile = NOT_RUN.** Offline data checks = PASS (see "Executed checks").

API facts I checked on the web (search only; dev.epicgames.com could not be fetched from this sandbox):
- `UCharacterMovementComponent::SetCrouchedHalfHeight(float)` exists. Documented 5.1 and 5.5; the direct property is deprecated in 5.4. Fine through at least 5.5.
- `ULocalLightComponent::SetIntensityUnits(ELightUnits)` exists. Documented 4.27, 5.1, 5.3 and 5.5.
- `USoundWave::SetSampleRate(uint32)` exists (5.4 docs). An unversioned page shows an extra `bFromDecoders` parameter, so check the defaulted second argument on 5.8.

- Not verified (from memory, low risk): `FScreenshotRequest::RequestScreenshot(FString,bool,bool)`, `FApp::GetGraphicsRHI()`, `FPlatformMisc::RequestExitWithStatus(bool,uint8)`, `EAutomationTestFlags::*` (both the old namespace enum and the 5.5+ enum class accept the `|` expression used), `OnSoundWaveProceduralUnderflow`, and the `FSoundAttenuationSettings` occlusion fields.

## Design red-team (greybox data only; no visuals exist)

- **Objective without documents:** readable. The HUD shows the objective at start (`ConfessionGameMode.cpp:110`, HUD fades it to 35% rather than hiding it). Objectives are spatial and short. The door's no-item message names the missing item. OK.
- **Canon text:** the work order and the counter notice are verbatim. Deviations are listed in R-25 and R-26.
- **Empty-room problem:** the lobby is dressed (counter, shelves, notice, frame). The service landing behind `Door_Basement_01` is six boxes and ends in a text card ("stair gate not built"), which is acceptable as a slice end but is the emptiest space. The east shelf bays hold the only optional content (the frame), which is off the lit route (R-11).
- **"Reject once then release":** the mechanic is there, but the explanation is thin. The only explanation is a 1.4 s line (R-06), the prompt vanishes (R-07), and "21-46" only means something if the player noticed that their order is 21-47 in the auto-opened document. Proposed falsification test: give 5 naive players the slice with no briefing. If 3 or more cannot say afterwards "the reader showed someone else's/the previous number before letting me in", the beat is not delivering its meaning and the release delay and text design must change.
- **Generic AI-horror clichés:** "没有人说话。" (R-25b) is the clearest. The frame that moves when unobserved is a known trope, but it is canon here and under-delivered rather than overdone (R-11). There are no jump-scares, meters or philosophy subtitles, which is good.

## Could NOT evaluate

1. Actual compilation against UE 5.4-5.8 (no engine). UHT acceptance of the nested non-reflected `struct FIntervalEmitter` (`GreyboxBuilder.h:38`) and `enum class EPhase` (`ConfessionAutoPilot.h:33`) inside UCLASS bodies is believed fine (UWorld has a nested struct) but is unverified. Also unverified: `MapKey`, `SetSampleRate` and `EAutomationTestFlags` exact signatures on 5.8.
2. Any PIE or `-game` run: spawn order (I traced InitGame, then builder spawn, then RestartPlayer at PostLogin, then StartPlay/BeginPlay, then build; it looks correct on paper), deferred-spawn component placement, door pose, focus sweep hits, checkpoint overlaps, autopilot timing, screenshots, HUD Slate lifetime. All NOT_RUN.
3. Whether `/Engine/Maps/Entry` carries a WorldSettings GameMode override that would bypass `GlobalDefaultGameMode`.
4. Audio behaviour of `USoundWaveProcedural` (underflow looping, `StartTime` on procedural sources, the self-occlusion in R-08) and the thread-safety of `QueueAudio` from the underflow callback in 5.8.
5. CJK glyph coverage of the default Slate font in a packaged build (PIE has the engine fallback font).
6. Lighting and exposure readability, Lumen behaviour on runtime-created Static primitives, frame time. No renders were evaluated.
7. Fun, fear, comprehension: needs human playtests (see the falsification test above).
8. Rights and disclosure of the 19 WAVs (origin is not stated in the files I reviewed).
